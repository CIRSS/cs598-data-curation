## Tentative Graded Assessments

Graded assessments will likely include:

* Weekly module quizzes
* Proctored midterm and final exams
* Five graded assignments

### Tentative Assignments

The goal for this semester is to use Databricks (Free Edition) as the primary platform for most assignments with outputs auto-graded via PrairieLearn. Assignments are being finalized, but will likely include:

| # | Assignment | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dataset Audit | Structured audit of real-world datasets based on course framework. |
| 2 | Data Acquisition and Storage | Ingest data into governed Databricks structures. |
| 3 | Data Documentation and Quality | Apply basic metadata; assess and enforce quality in Databricks. |
| 4 | Workflow Automation and Lineage | Automate pipeline; apply lineage information. |
| 5 | Privacy Assessment and Remediation | Assess reidentification risk; remediate identified issues. |
